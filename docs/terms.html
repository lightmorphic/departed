Your responsibilities

You encrypt your own archive and you choose your own passphrase. If you lose that passphrase, the archive cannot be recovered, by us or by anyone. If you give the passphrase to the wrong person, they can open the archive. If you leave the program reachable from the open internet, other people may find it. Those choices are yours.

The letter you write inside the program is not protected the way your files are. The program has to be able to read it in order to send it, so anyone who takes the whole machine can read it too. Keep anything private inside the locked files instead.

If you run it for other people, you are the one holding their accounts. Removing an account deletes that person's files, settings and history, and there is no undo.
